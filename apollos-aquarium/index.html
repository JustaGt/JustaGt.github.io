05FAQ

Common questions

How often should my tank be serviced?

Most freshwater tanks do well every two weeks. Reef systems and heavily stocked tanks usually need weekly care. We recommend a schedule after the first visit.

Do I need to be home during visits?

No. Many clients provide a key or entry code. Notes are sent after every visit so you know exactly what was done.

Do you bring water and supplies?

Yes. Conditioned water, test kits and cleaning supplies are included. Filter media and specialty additives are billed at cost.

Do you service businesses?

Yes. Offices, lobbies, restaurants, clinics and retail. Larger and commercial systems are quoted after a site visit.

Do you take care of ponds?

Yes. Koi ponds and water features receive cleaning, pump and filter service and seasonal water checks.
